**Voltage Gain**
$A_v=\dfrac{v_o}{v_i}$
$A_{v(dB)}=20log_{10}(|{A_v}|)\text{dB}$

**Current Gain**
$A_i=\dfrac{i_o}{i_i}$
$A_{i(dB)}=20log_{10}(|{A_I}|)\text{dB}$

**Power Gain**
Power amplifiers tend to amplify current rather than voltage
$A_p=\dfrac{P_L}{P_I}=\dfrac{v_oi_o}{v_ii_i}=A_vA_i$
$A_{p(dB)}=10log_{10}(|{A_p}|)\text{dB}$
